FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Book a free 15-minute call and ask anything.

How can you launch in 5 days?

The process is fixed and booked in advance, and I take a small number of projects each month so yours gets my full focus. The 5 working days start once I have your content, logins and branding. Launch and Signature sites go live within 5 working days. Flagship sites take 10–15 working days because of their shop and custom features.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

On Signature and Flagship, yes. You get an easy editor for your pages, plus your blog on Signature and your online shop on Flagship. Launch sites are kept simple, so small changes are handled for you through your care plan.

What do you need from me to start?

Nothing to begin with. Tell me about your business and I'll design a free demo of your new website. If you love it, you pay the 50% deposit and send your logo, text and any photos. If you need new photos, I can shoot them for you.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. Everything runs remotely, and calls are scheduled around your time zone.

What happens after launch?

You own everything. You can stay on a care plan for updates and backups, or take it from there yourself.

Can you photograph our team or products without a website project?

Yes. Every photography service can be booked on its own. Booked with a new website, shoots cost less. For example, a half-day brand shoot drops from £795 to £595.
