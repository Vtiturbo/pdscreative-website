03 / SIGNATURE STYLE
Signature style · what is cybersigilism

Ancient magic, rewired.

Cybersigilism (sy-ber-sij-il-ism) fuses the sigil — a symbol believed to hold occult power — with the cold precision of circuitry, biomechanics and Y2K chrome. Think tribal, reborn as hairline-thin, razor-sharp linework.

'90s
Tribal era

Thick, bold black "tribal" flames and blades dominate the Y2K tattoo world — the style cybersigilism would later reinvent.

Late '10s
The birth

Underground artists begin reworking sigils with cybernetic, mechanical bends. Berlin's techno and rave scene becomes its spiritual home.

Name
It gets a name

Artists in LA, New York and Mexico City push the look; "cybersigilism" sticks as the name for this sharp, spooky, sensual linework.

Icons
Celebrity ink

Musicians and pop stars wear the style — including a famous white-ink sternum piece — and it explodes across Instagram.

Now
Gen Z's tattoo

TikTok crowns it the defining tattoo of a generation. Clients now arrive informed, intentional, and looking for true specialists.

Line

Razor-fine

Thin, consistent black lines that swell and taper like a calligrapher's stroke. Nowhere to hide — every wobble shows, so execution is everything.

Form

Perfect symmetry

Mirrored, bilateral designs built around a central axis — a spine, a sternum, a vertebra line — with thorns and barbs fanning outward.

Flow

Body-hugging

Designs wrap, weave and move with your anatomy. They live on long curved planes: spine, sternum, forearm, neck, hip and hand.

09 / HEALING
Healing timeline

Heal it sharp

Fine lines need careful healing. Here's what's normal at each stage — and exactly how to look after your new sigil.

  1. Leave the film onSecond-skin film stays on for 3–5 days. Some ink and plasma under it is normal. Peel it off slowly in a warm shower.
  2. Wash gently, twice a dayFragrance-free soap, clean hands, lukewarm water. Pat dry with clean kitchen roll — never a towel.
  3. Thin layer of balmA rice-grain amount, rubbed in until it disappears. Too much suffocates the skin and softens fine lines.
  4. Let it breatheLoose, clean cotton over the area. Change bedsheets the night before and after your session.
  5. Hands off the flakesPeeling and itching are normal. Don't pick or scratch — tap it lightly or apply balm instead.
  6. Send a healed photoAt around 6 weeks, in daylight. Touch-ups within 3 months are free.

12 / FAQ
FAQ & policies

Before you ask

Still unsure? Send a message with the booking form and I'll answer personally.

What styles do you tattoo?

Four: cybersigilism and neo-tribal thorned webs (the signature), gothic blackletter lettering, black & grey realism (horror is a favourite) and fine-line illustrative dark art. If your idea sits outside these, ask — I'll tell you honestly if another artist would suit it better.

Can you copy a design I found online?

I won't copy another artist's work. Bring it as a reference and I'll draw an original piece with the same energy, designed for your body.

Will the symmetry hold on a curved area?

Yes — that's why I draw on the body. Some designs need small adaptations so they look symmetrical when you move; I'll explain each one before we start.

Does fine linework blur over time?

All tattoos soften slightly with age. Lines are spaced so they age cleanly; sun protection and good aftercare matter most. Every healed result is in the portfolio for that reason.

How much will it hurt?

Linework is usually more comfortable than heavy realism shading. Bony areas — spine, sternum, ribs, hands — are more intense. Check the placement guide for each zone.

Can I use numbing cream?

Ask first. Some creams change how skin takes ink, so if you want to use one we'll agree the product and timing before your session.

Can you tattoo over scars or old work?

Often, yes — sigils can weave around scars beautifully. Fully healed scars (usually 12+ months) are best. Fine lines can't cover dark old tattoos, but can frame them.

Deposit & cancellation policy

Deposits are non-refundable but can be moved once with 72 hours' notice. Missing a session without notice forfeits the deposit.

Can I bring a friend?

One guest is welcome. The space is small and calm, so no groups please.
